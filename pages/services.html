Our Services

Adopt

Adoptions cover cats, dogs, and small caged animals including lizards, rabbits, etc. Animals on site change often, call or come in person to meet your new best friend. Every adopted animal is spayed or neutered, vaccinated, and health-checked before going home, and our volunteers will tell you honestly what each animal is like so you can choose a match that suits your household. If you fall for someone here, reach out through the Contact form and we will help with the next steps.

Volunteer

Click a position to add an information request to the contact form

Take our dogs out for walks and playtime, giving them exercise, fresh air, and the socialization that helps them get adopted.

Spend time with our cats, especially the shy ones, so they get comfortable with people and show their real personalities to adopters.

Help run adoption events by setting up, greeting visitors, answering questions about the animals, and handling animals at the event.

Foster

Experienced fosters are needed. Fosters give an animal a temporary home while it waits for adoption - a quiet place to recover and show its real personality. Most stays last a few weeks. We cover food, supplies, and veterinary care; you give daily care, photos and updates, and rides to vet visits or events. Every animal in a foster home frees up space for another animal in need. If you have experience and room in your home, send your interest through the Contact form.
