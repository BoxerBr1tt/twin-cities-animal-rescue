Welcome to Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue gives animals with nowhere else to go medical care, time to recover, and a path to a permanent home. Our volunteers, fosters and adopters are found throughout Minneapolis and Saint Paul.

How You Can Help

Adopt

Help give an animal in need its forever home

Foster

Help prepare animals for their forever homes

Volunteer

Make a real difference

Our Impact

Since 1999, Twin Cities Animal Rescue has helped animals across Minneapolis and Saint Paul find loving homes, thanks to a volunteer team and foster homes drawn from the very community we serve.
